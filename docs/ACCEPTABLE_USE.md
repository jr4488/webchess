# WebChess acceptable use policy

**Effective date:** July 26, 2026

WebChess may be used for lawful, low- or moderate-stakes reflection and
research. It may not be used to harm people, evade controls, or present
generated salience as verified evidence.

## Prohibited use

Do not use WebChess to:

- break the law or help another person break the law;
- threaten, harass, exploit, discriminate against, or deceive a person;
- generate or facilitate malware, credential theft, unauthorized access,
  denial of service, spam, scraping abuse, or circumvention of security;
- submit secrets, authentication artifacts, regulated data, confidential
  material, children's data, or third-party personal data without authority;
- make or automate medical, legal, financial, employment, education, housing,
  insurance, credit, safety, eligibility, law-enforcement, or other
  consequential decisions about a person;
- impersonate a person or misrepresent model output as human expertise,
  objective evidence, prediction, or a WebChess-validated fact;
- evade division, Portia, Gate, Retry, Charlotte, Wilbur, replay, move, export,
  model, rate, quota, suspension,
  deletion, account, or cost controls, including through duplicate identities,
  idempotency-key manipulation, or network-identity rotation;
- share, sell, or automate accounts;
- probe or overload the service except through an explicitly authorized
  security test; or
- use model or user text to instruct the application to disclose secrets,
  change ownership, bypass replay, alter usage records, or invoke unauthorized
  tools.

## Responsible research

Reproducible criticism, red-team testing, and security research are welcome
when they use non-sensitive fixtures, stay within an explicitly authorized
scope, avoid accessing another user's data, minimize service impact, and report
vulnerabilities privately through [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

WebChess may reject a request, reduce limits, temporarily block activity,
suspend an account, delete content, or terminate access when reasonably needed
to protect users, providers, the project, or the public.

Non-sensitive policy questions belong in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/jr4488/webchess/discussions).
